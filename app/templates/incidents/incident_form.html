Operating procedure
  1. File when first aware — the department is notified immediately.
  2. Triage within the severity SLA: critical 1h, high 4h, medium 24h, low 72h.
  3. Contain, then mitigate, then close — closure requires a root cause.
  4. Personal-data breaches must reach the supervisory authority within 72h of awareness (Art. 33) — tick “regulatory notification required” and record when it was sent.
  5. NIS2 significant incidents also owe three reports — early warning at 24h, notification at 72h, final report at 30 days. Tick the NIS2 box and record each filing as it happens.
