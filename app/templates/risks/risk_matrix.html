How to read these matrices
  • Left matrix — position each risk by its inherent likelihood × impact.
  • Right matrix — same position, but the cell colour reflects the residual score after controls.
  • Click any populated cell — filters the risk register to those risks.
  • Hover any cell — a + button appears in the corner to add a new risk at that exact L×I.
  • Yellow ! badge — that cell contains unassessed risks.
Adding risks
  • Use + New Risk for a blank form.
  • Use a cell's + button to create a risk pre-set to that L×I.
  • Newly created risks start as Identified — mark them Assessed once confirmed.
  • Attach controls and create treatments to lower the residual score on the right.
