When to use risk exceptions

Use a formal exception when:

  • A risk cannot be mitigated within appetite
  • Remediation is deferred due to business constraints
  • Risk is consciously accepted by management

Every exception must have an approver, an expiry date, and a justification. When it expires, the risk must be re-evaluated.
